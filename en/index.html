The training

Training acroyoga teachers since 2017

A space to go deeper together into a practice that develops both physical skills and our capacity to connect: positive communication, present listening and openness.

Teaching is at our core. We believe that with strong teaching foundations and personal work, an acroyoga teacher can grow and create as much as they set out to.

Community is the foundation of the practice. We have watched acroyoga grow from its beginnings and learned, through mistakes and successes, what the pillars of a free, creative, united and inspiring community are. We share all of it with you so you can find your own path.

We love helping you find your own voice as a teacher. Much of the training focuses on reflecting back your gifts and talents, and embracing your shadows, so you leave with the confidence to share yourself with the world.

Curriculum

Three paths,
one training

Teaching skills to share the practice, technique to fly, and the therapeutic approach that makes acroyoga a practice in service of what makes us human.

01

Teaching

  • Verbal and non-verbal communication
  • Learning styles
  • Sequencing: regular classes, workshops and festivals
  • Content for beginner, intermediate and advanced levels
  • Human dynamics: games and connection exercises
  • Yoga, philosophy and asanas
  • Marketing and abundance applied to acroyoga
02

Acrobatic practice

  • Acrobatic flying: washing machines and flows
  • Icarians and pops
  • Two-high: principles and safety
  • Handstands
  • Movement, animal flow and movement flow
03

Therapeutic practice

  • Therapeutic flying: sequences, principles and exploration
  • Thai massage and dynamic Thai massage
  • Partner yoga

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

Do I need previous experience?

No. We approach the practice step by step and offer levels so everyone can enjoy the experience and get the most out of it.

What does the price include?

Accommodation (camping or a shared room at the guesthouse), 3 daily meals and the full professional training, with the PDF manual, one-on-one mentoring and a certificate.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. The certificate is awarded once you complete the full training.

What time does it start and end?

We begin on February 3 at 2 pm and say goodbye on February 14 at 3 pm, after lunch.

How do the deposit and installments work?

A USD 300 deposit locks in the price. You pay the rest in fixed monthly installments, between the 1st and the 10th of each month, and the final one must be paid by February 3, no exceptions. If you pay everything at once, you get USD 100 off.

How do I pay from another country?

Through ARQ (formerly Dollar App) or Western Union. Fees from the platform you choose are paid by you.

Can I pay in Argentine pesos?

Yes. Prices are in US dollars, but you can pay the equivalent in pesos at the exchange rate on the day of payment.

What if I can’t make it?

If you cancel your participation, payments are non-refundable. The training cannot be exchanged for another one or transferred to another person.
